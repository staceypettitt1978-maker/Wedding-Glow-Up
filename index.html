    Pre‑Cruise: 5 Days in Rome

    Flights: Emirates (BNE → FCO)

    ▾

    Rome itinerary (relaxed, senior-friendly)

    Hotel options (2 rooms): Singer Palace (A$1,800–A$2,500), Hotel de la Ville (A$2,800–A$4,000+), Hotel Artemide (A$1,200–A$1,800). Recommendation: Hotel Artemide for value, elevator access, and proximity to Monti. Hotel Artemide

    Day 1: Arrival & Trastevere

    • Private transfer FCO → hotel (≈ €60; A$100–A$110)
    • Orientation walk (Trevi or Spanish Steps)
    • Twilight Trastevere Food Tour — A$162 pp

    Day 2: Colosseum & Monti

    • Colosseum Arena Floor (semi-private) — A$135 pp
    • Lunch in Monti (short taxi)
    • Mandatory rest
    • Dinner: Da Armando al Pantheon (book early)

    Day 3: Golf cart & art

    • Private Golf Cart Tour — A$490–A$655 total (4 people)
    • Lunch: Campo de’ Fiori market
    • Palazzo Doria Pamphilj (quiet, Caravaggio, Velázquez, Raphael)

    Day 4: Vatican & Prati

    • Vatican Early Access — A$195 pp (with passage to St. Peter’s)
    • Lunch in Prati (Via Cola di Rienzo)
    • Optional: Dome climb (Carl & Stacey)
    • Farewell dinner with a view (Aroma, Terrazza Borromini, Casina Valadier)

    Day 5: Leisure & port transfer

    • Cappuccino at Sant’Eustachio il Caffè, souvenir time
    • Private transfer to Civitavecchia — A$260–A$375 total (RomeCabs)

    Hidden gems & calm escapes

    • Aventine Hill: Orange Garden & Knights of Malta Keyhole
    • Basilica di San Clemente: layered archaeology
    • Appian Way: ancient road, catacombs, pine-lined tranquility

      Core & Stomach

      Equipment: Pilates ball, yoga mat, Arena bands, SPOR bench

      • Pilates Ball Toe Taps (3x12): Lie on back, ball under lower back, knees at 90°. Lower one foot to tap the floor, return, alternate. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Arena Band Dead Bugs (3x10): Lie on back, hold band overhead, knees bent. Extend opposite arm and leg. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Mini Reformer Leg Circles (2x10 each way): Feet in straps. Draw controlled circles outward and inward. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • SPOR Bench Incline Crunches (2x15): Lie on incline bench, feet anchored. Curl torso up, exhale, lower slowly. ▶️ Search Form Video

      Arms & Shoulders

      Equipment: Dumbbells (light), SPOR bench, Arena bands

      • Band Pull-Aparts (3x15): Stand holding band. Pull hands apart, squeezing shoulder blades. Crucial for shoulder stability. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Incline Push-ups on Bench (3x10): Hands on bench edge. Lower chest to bench. Easier on shoulders. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Dumbbell Hammer Curls (3x12): Sit or stand, palms facing in. Curl weights up without rotating wrists. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Seated Band Rows (3x12): Sit on floor, band around feet. Pull handles towards chest, squeezing back. ▶️ Search Form Video

      Butt & Thighs

      Equipment: Mini reformer, Arena booty cards, yoga mat

      • Arena Booty Band Glute Bridges (3x15): Band above knees. Lie on back, lift hips, squeeze glutes. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Side-Lying Leg Lifts (3x15 each side): Lie on side, legs stacked. Lift top leg slowly, control the movement down. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Mini Reformer Footwork (3x20): Various foot positions (heels, toes, wide) to press the carriage out. ▶️ Search Form Video

      Mobility & Stretch

      Equipment: Arena stretch cards, Pilates ball, yoga mat

      Use your Arena Strength Stretch & Mobility Cards. Focus on flowing through shoulder rolls, cat-cow, hip openers, and band-assisted stretches. This is a vital recovery and shoulder stability day.

      Full Body Circuit

      Equipment: Arena body cards, dumbbells, SPOR bench, bands

      Perform each exercise back-to-back for 1 round. Rest 60-90 seconds. Complete 3 rounds total.

      • Dumbbell Goblet Squats (12 reps): Hold one dumbbell vertically against your chest. Squat down, keeping your chest up. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Incline Push-ups on Bench (10 reps): Shoulder-safe way to build chest and arm strength. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Seated Band Rows (12 reps): Focus on squeezing your back muscles. ▶️ Search Form Video
      • Glute Bridges (20 reps): Can be done with or without a band. ▶️ Search Form Video
